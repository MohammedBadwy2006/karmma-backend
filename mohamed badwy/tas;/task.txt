Problem 1 — Infix to Postfix Conversion
Convert the following infix expression to postfix using the stack
algorithm. show the full trace — the stack state after every character is
processed.
● A * (B + C) ^ D - E
Solution:-
Output: A , Stack: [* , (]
Output: AB, Stack: [* , ( , +]
We reach ‘)’ , so pop till ‘(‘
Output: ABC+ , Stack: [*]
^ is higher precedence than * , Stack: [* , ^]
Pop all -&gt; Output: ABC+D^*
Stack: [-]
Output: ABC+D^*E
Pop [-]
Final Output: ABC+D^*E-

Problem 2 — Postfix Expression Evaluation
Evaluate the following postfix expression using a stack. Show the stack
state after every step.
● 7 5 3 * 5 1 ^ / + 3 2 - +
Solution:-
Push [7, 5, 3] , Stack:[7, 5, 3]
‘*’ -&gt; Output: 5 * 3 = 15, Stack:[7, 15]
Push [5, 1] ,Stack:[7, 15, 5, 1]
‘^’ -&gt; Output: 5 ^ 1 = 5 , Stack:[7, 15, 5]
‘/’ -&gt; Output: 15 / 5 = 3, Stack:[7, 3]
‘+’ -&gt; Output: 7 + 3 = 10, Stack:[10]
Push [3, 2] , Stack [10, 3, 2]
‘-’ -&gt; Output: 3 - 1 = 1, Stack:[10, 1]
Output: 10 + 1 = 11
Final Output: 11
